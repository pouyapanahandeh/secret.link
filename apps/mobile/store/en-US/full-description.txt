Secretlink is a privacy-first app for sharing secrets that disappear.

Send end-to-end encrypted messages to @handles. Drop ephemeral stories on the map. Everything expires — no permanent inbox, no endless scroll of your past.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Create an @handle and a public profile others can find
• Send encrypted secrets that self-destruct after views or time
• Drop location stories on the map for people nearby to reveal
• Follow people you care about and share your profile link
• Control your data — delete your account anytime

BUILT FOR PRIVACY
• End-to-end encryption for secret content
• Ephemeral by default — secrets expire
• No ads, no trackers in the product experience
• Clear privacy policy: https://secretlink.life/privacy

WHO IT'S FOR
Adults 18+ who want a lighter, more private way to say something real — then let it go.
